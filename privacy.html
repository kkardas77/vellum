Privacy Policy

The administrator of your personal data is Vellum Records. For privacy-related questions, contact us at: hello@vellumrecords.com

Your email address is used solely to notify you about upcoming Vellum Records releases, drops, and announcements. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Processing is based on your voluntary consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time.

Your email address is stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet, secured by Google's infrastructure. We retain your data until you request its removal or withdraw your consent.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Google LLC acts as a data processor under Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to: access your data, correct it, request its deletion, restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@vellumrecords.com

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools. No third-party advertising or tracking scripts are active.
